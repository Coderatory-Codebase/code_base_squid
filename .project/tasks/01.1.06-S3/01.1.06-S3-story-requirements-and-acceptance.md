# Sprint 1 · Story 4 — 01.1.06-S3

## Story

**A deleted organization’s data is removed from every module after 30 days.**

Squid places this as the fourth story in Muhammad Salman’s Sprint 1 “My work” list. Its canonical Squid record is `01.1.06-S3` (the suffix is S3 even though it is the fourth visible story in the personal sprint list).

## What the story means

An organization deletion request schedules a purge date at least 30 days later. On the first daily run at or after that date, Workspace starts a purge for every workspace and publishes `WorkspaceDeleted` once per workspace. Workspace may remove its organization, workspace and membership documents and mark the organization `DELETED` only after every data-holding module confirms its purge. A privacy-safe trace records identifiers and confirmation times only. The purge must start within 24 hours of its deletion date, finish within 24 hours when all modules are healthy, and alert if it is still only `DELETION_SCHEDULED` more than 31 days after the request.
