## Implementation

- The central ability `organization.settings.update` is declared in
  `servers/api/features/access/policy/abilities.ts`.
- Workspace binds `workspace.organization-settings.update` to that ability in
  `servers/api/features/workspace/policies/organization-settings.policy.ts`.

  bus refuses commands with no attached policy decision before a handler runs.
